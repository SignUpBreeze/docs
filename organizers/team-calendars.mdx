Team calendars are built for sports coaches and team managers. Connect your team's PlayMetrics calendar once, and SignUpBreeze keeps a sign-up page in sync for every game — so parents can claim drinks and snacks without you lifting a finger for the rest of the season.

You paste your calendar link, choose your team's look, and share a single page. New games appear on their own, schedule changes flow through automatically, and you never have to create an event by hand.

<Note>
  A team calendar replaces manual event creation. When you set up a calendar-driven team, your games are managed entirely by the feed — the usual "Create event" tools are hidden for that team.
</Note>

## Getting started

Team calendars are built for sports teams and clubs that manage their schedule in **PlayMetrics**. To get set up, reach out to us at [hello@signupbreeze.com](mailto:hello@signupbreeze.com) and we'll enable a calendar-driven team for you.

Once your team is ready, you're taken straight to the **Calendar** settings to connect your feed — covered next.

## Connecting your PlayMetrics feed

Open your team's settings and select the **Calendar** tab. Fill in:

- **PlayMetrics calendar URL** — paste your team's calendar link. The dedicated games-only feed is recommended, but any of your team's PlayMetrics calendar links will work — SignUpBreeze always filters down to games.
- **Sport** — choose your sport.
- **Theme** — pick one of the preset color themes. It's applied to your public team page and to every game's sign-up page.
- **Team logo** — upload a logo to show at the top of your team page.

Click **Connect & sync**. SignUpBreeze immediately checks the feed and pulls in your games. If the link isn't a valid calendar URL, you'll see an error and nothing is saved — just fix the link and try again.

<Note>
  In PlayMetrics, open your team's schedule and look for the calendar subscription (`.ics`) link. Copying the games-only feed keeps practices out from the start, though SignUpBreeze ignores practices either way.
</Note>

## How games appear

Each game in your feed becomes its own event:

- The title reads **vs Opponent** for home games and **@ Opponent** for away games.
- A **Game** badge marks it as a game.
- The date, time, location, and details (arrival time, uniform, venue) come straight from the feed.
- Two sign-up slots are added automatically — **Drinks** and **Snacks** — each for one person.

Practices and other non-game items on the feed are skipped, so your team page stays focused on games.

## Keeping games in sync

Your calendar stays current on its own:

- **Daily sync** — SignUpBreeze checks your feed every day and applies any changes.
- **Sync now** — need an update right away? Use the **Sync now** button on the Calendar settings tab.

When something changes in the feed, SignUpBreeze updates the matching game in place:

- **Reschedules** — if a game's time or location changes, the event is updated and any parents who already signed up are emailed about the change. Their sign-ups are kept.
- **Cancellations** — if a game drops off the feed, its page is taken down and signed-up parents are notified. If that game later returns to the feed, its page comes back automatically.

The **Calendar** tab shows when your feed last synced so you can confirm everything is up to date.

<Note>
  A feed that briefly returns no games won't wipe your schedule — SignUpBreeze leaves your games in place and flags the anomaly instead of cancelling everything.
</Note>

## Sharing your team page

Your team has one public page that lists all upcoming games. Share its link at the start of the season and you're done — it always reflects the latest schedule.

The URL looks like: `signupbreeze.com/t/atlanta-fire-bu15`

Send it however you reach your team's families — group chat, email, a team app, or a season welcome message.

### What parents see

On the team page, families see your logo and team name, then every upcoming game with its date, time, opponent, and venue. Each game shows whether **Drinks** and **Snacks** are still open or who's already bringing them.

To sign up, a parent taps a game, which opens its sign-up page, and claims a slot — no account needed. They confirm with their name and email and get a magic link to manage their sign-up later, exactly like any other SignUpBreeze event.

Past games drop off the page automatically, so the list always shows what's coming up next.
