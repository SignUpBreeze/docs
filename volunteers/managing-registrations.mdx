---
title: Managing Your Registrations
description: How to view and manage your volunteer signups.
---

After signing up for a shift, you can manage your registration using the magic link sent to your email.

## Viewing your registration

Click the magic link in your confirmation email to see your registration details, including the event name, shift time, and location.

## Confirmation page

The confirmation page shows everything about your signup:

- **Event details** — title, description, and location
- **Your shift** — the date and time you signed up for
- **Registration status** — confirmation that you're signed up

## Tips

- **Save the confirmation email** — it contains your magic link for easy access later.
- **Check the event details before your shift** — organizers may update the description with last-minute information like parking directions or what to bring.
