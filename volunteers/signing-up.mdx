---
title: Signing Up for an Event
description: How to sign up for a volunteer shift — no account needed.
---

Signing up for a volunteer shift on SignUpBreeze takes about 30 seconds. You don't need to create an account.

## How to sign up

1. **Open the event link** — you'll get this from the organizer via email, text, social media, or by scanning a QR code.
2. **Browse available shifts** — the event page shows all shifts with dates, times, and how many spots are left.
3. **Pick a shift** — tap on the one that works for you.
4. **Enter your info** — just your name and email address.
5. **Confirm** — you'll see a confirmation page and receive a magic link via email.

That's it. No password to create, no account to manage.

## What you'll see

The public event page shows the event title, description, location, and a list of available shifts. Shifts that are full will be marked as such. You can only sign up for shifts that still have open spots.

## After signing up

You'll be taken to a confirmation page that shows your registration details. You'll also receive an email with a magic link that you can use to return to your confirmation at any time.
