---
title: What is SignUpBreeze?
description: A modern, mobile-first volunteer signup platform that's clean, fast, and ad-free.
---

SignUpBreeze is a modern alternative to legacy signup tools like SignUpGenius. It's built for organizers who want a clean, ad-free experience and volunteers who want to sign up in seconds from their phone.

## Who it's for

**Organizers** create events with time-based shifts and share a single link. No spreadsheets, no back-and-forth emails.

**Volunteers** tap a link, pick a shift, and they're signed up. No account required — magic links handle everything securely.

## What makes it different

- **Mobile-first** — designed for phones first, because that's where volunteers are when they sign up.
- **Ad-free** — no banner ads, no pop-ups, no distractions. Ever.
- **No account required for volunteers** — magic links let people sign up without creating a password or remembering yet another login.
- **Team workspaces** — organizers can collaborate on events within an organization, not just solo.
- **QR codes** — every event gets a scannable QR code for flyers, bulletins, and handouts.

## How it works

1. An organizer creates an event and adds shifts (date, time, number of spots).
2. They share the public event link or QR code.
3. Volunteers visit the page, pick a shift, and confirm with their name and email.
4. Everyone gets a confirmation — the volunteer via magic link, the organizer on their dashboard.

That's it. No friction, no clutter.
