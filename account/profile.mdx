title: Profile Settings
description: How to update your name, email, timezone, organization name, and delete your account.
---

Your profile settings let you manage your personal information and your organization.

## Updating your profile

Go to **Settings > Profile** to update the following:

- **Name** — your display name across SignUpBreeze
- **Email** — the email address associated with your account
- **Timezone** — used as the default timezone when you create new events

Click **Save** to apply your changes.

## Organization name

Below your profile fields, you'll find an **Organization** section where you can update your organization name. This name is displayed across the platform wherever your organization appears.

## Deleting your account

At the bottom of the Profile page, click the **Delete account** button to permanently delete your account and all of its resources. This action cannot be undone.
