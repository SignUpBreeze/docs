---
title: Signing In & Security
description: How to sign in to SignUpBreeze with a one-time email code or a social account.
---

SignUpBreeze is **passwordless** — there's no password to create, remember, or reset. You sign in with a one-time code sent to your email, or with a Google or Apple account.

## Signing in with an email code

1. Go to the sign-in page and enter your **email address**.
2. We email you a **6-digit code**. Open your inbox and copy it.
3. Enter the code to sign in.

If this is your first time, you'll be asked for your name (and, optionally, an organization name) to finish creating your account — all in the same flow.

<Note>Codes are single-use and expire after a short time. If yours doesn't arrive, check your spam folder or request a new one. Because there's no password, no one can sign in without access to your email.</Note>

## Signing in with Google or Apple

Prefer one tap? Choose **Continue with Google** or **Continue with Apple** on the sign-in page. You'll authenticate with that provider and land back in SignUpBreeze. If you don't have an account yet, one is created for you using the name and email from that provider.

You can use the email code and social sign-in interchangeably, as long as they share the same email address.

## Confirming your identity

When you open a sensitive part of your account, SignUpBreeze may ask you to confirm it's really you by entering a fresh code emailed to you. This keeps your account protected even if you step away from a signed-in device.
