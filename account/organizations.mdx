Organizations let you create shared workspaces where multiple people can manage events together.

## Switching organizations

Every account belongs to at least one organization. The organization switcher sits at the top of the sidebar and shows the name of the one you're currently working in.

Click the switcher to:

- **Switch organizations** — select any organization you belong to. A checkmark marks your current one. You'll land on a valid page for that organization.
- **Create a new organization** — click **New organization**, give it a name, and click **Create organization**. You'll be taken straight to the new organization's settings.

<Note>Each organization keeps its own events, shifts, and registrations separate. Switching changes which organization's data you see across the app.</Note>

## Renaming an organization

Go to **Settings > General**, update the **Organization name** field, and click **Save**. The name is displayed across the platform wherever your organization appears.

<Note>On a calendar-driven organization, this field reads **Team name** instead.</Note>

## Adding a logo

Go to **Settings > General** and find the **Logo** section, below the organization name.

1. Click **Organization logo** and choose an image.
2. Check the preview.
3. Click **Save logo**.

To change it, upload a different image and click **Save logo** again. To take it off, click **Remove logo**, then **Save logo**.

Use a square PNG, JPEG, or WebP image between 128×128 and 1024×1024 pixels, up to 2 MB. SignUpBreeze resizes and optimizes it for you.

On the Pro plan, once you [remove SignUpBreeze branding](#removing-signupbreeze-branding), your logo also appears at the top of the emails your volunteers receive.

Logos are available on every plan. Only people who can manage the organization's settings see the **Logo** section.

<Note>On a calendar-driven organization, upload your logo under **Settings > Calendar** instead. It shows at the top of your public team page. See [Team calendars](/organizers/team-calendars).</Note>

## Removing SignUpBreeze branding

On the Pro plan, the organization owner can remove SignUpBreeze branding from everything your volunteers see. Go to **Settings > General**, find the **Branding** section, and check **Remove SignUpBreeze branding**. The change saves right away.

With branding removed:

- Your public event, confirmation, and waitlist pages don't show "Powered by SignUpBreeze".
- Emails to your volunteers show your logo and name at the top, sign off as your organization, and leave out any SignUpBreeze promotion. They're sent as "Your organization via SignUpBreeze".

Branding stays on until the owner turns it off, including when you first upgrade to Pro. Uncheck the box to bring it back.

Admins and members can see the setting but can't change it. On the Free and Starter plans, the checkbox is grayed out. If you move from Pro to another plan, branding comes back automatically.

## Availability

Inviting others to collaborate is a paid feature. On the free plan, you'll see a prompt to join the waitlist when you visit **Settings > Organizations**.

Once available on your plan, you'll be able to invite members and manage them in one shared organization.
