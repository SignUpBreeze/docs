Manage your email notification preferences from **Settings > Notifications**.

## Daily Digest

The **Daily Digest** sends you a morning email summarizing sign-up activity across all your events over the last 24 hours. It's enabled by default.

Each digest includes:

- **A running total** of new sign-ups and cancellations across your events.
- **A per-event breakdown**, with each shift's fill count (for example, "3/5") and a **+N today** marker for new sign-ups. Full shifts are flagged **FULL**.
- **Direct links** to each event and to your full dashboard.

Toggle the digest on or off from **Settings > Notifications** and click **Save** to update your preference. Every digest email also includes a link to change this setting.
