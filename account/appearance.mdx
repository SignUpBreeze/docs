SignUpBreeze supports light and dark themes so you can work in the environment that's most comfortable for you.

## Changing your theme

Go to **Settings > Appearance** to choose from three options:

- **Light** — a bright, white background
- **Dark** — a dark background that's easier on the eyes in low-light environments
- **System** — automatically follows your device's light/dark mode setting

Your theme preference is saved to your account and applies across all devices.
