<Update label="November 2026" description="Organization logos, and removing SignUpBreeze branding on Pro">
  ### Add your organization's logo
  Every organization, on every plan, can now upload a logo. Go to **Settings > General**, choose an image in the **Logo** section, and click **Save logo**. You can replace or remove it there at any time. See [adding a logo](/account/organizations#adding-a-logo).

  ### Remove SignUpBreeze branding on Pro
  On the Pro plan, your organization's owner can remove SignUpBreeze branding. Check **Remove SignUpBreeze branding** under **Settings > General**, and your public event, confirmation and waitlist pages stop showing any SignUpBreeze branding. Volunteers see your event and your organization. Branding stays on until the owner turns it off. See [removing SignUpBreeze branding](/account/organizations#removing-signupbreeze-branding).

  With branding removed, emails to your volunteers are branded as your organization too. They show your logo and name at the top, sign off as your organization, and leave out any SignUpBreeze promotion. They're sent as "Your organization via SignUpBreeze", and volunteers' replies go to your organization's owner.

  ### Fixes
  - Signing in with Apple works reliably again.
  - The **Event Stations & Booths** template shows its cover image again, including on events already created from it.
</Update>

<Update label="October 2026" description="Add to calendar and directions in volunteer emails">
  ### Add to calendar
  Confirmation and reminder emails now include **Add to calendar** links for every shift a volunteer signs up for, for Google Calendar or Apple and Outlook. The confirmation page has the same links. In Gmail and Apple Mail, the sign-up can also show up as a booking card with the date and place.

  ### Directions for every in-person shift
  Every in-person shift now gets a **Get Directions** link that opens maps on the volunteer's phone. If a shift is at a different address from the rest of the event, the link goes to that shift's address. A reminder covering shifts at several places links to each one.

  ### Back where you left off after signing in
  If you open a link to a page in SignUpBreeze while signed out, you now land on that page after signing in with your email code, instead of on your dashboard.
</Update>

<Update label="September 2026" description="Volunteers hear from you when an organization closes">
  ### Volunteers are told when an organization closes
  When you delete an organization or a sports team, everyone signed up for its upcoming events now gets an email letting them know those events are cancelled and their spot has been released. People on a waitlist are included too, so nobody is left waiting for a spot that will never open.

  Each person gets **one email** listing every event they were on, rather than a separate message per event. A volunteer signed up for a whole season of games hears about it once.
</Update>

<Update label="August 2026" description="A public API, in-app support, and a support tier on every plan">
  ### An API for your organization
  SignUpBreeze now has a public API, so other software can do what you would otherwise do by hand — create an event, add and resize its shifts, check what still needs volunteers, and read who signed up. Point your scripts, your automation platform, or an AI agent at it.

  Create a token under **Settings > API**, granting only the scopes the integration needs, and start with the [API introduction](/api-reference/introduction).

  ### Get help without leaving the app
  There's a new **Support** page at [Settings > Support](/account/support). On paid plans you can open a request, describe what happened, and follow the whole conversation in one place. We reply by email, and every reply shows up in the thread too.

  ### Support on every plan
  Every plan now names what support it includes — **Community** on Free, **Email** on Starter, and **Priority** on Pro, where your requests are triaged first. The documentation and the Support page are open to everyone, whatever plan you're on. See the [plan comparison](/account/billing) or [how to get help](/account/support).

  ### Sharper organization logos
  Logos are now resized and optimized when you upload them, so they load faster and look right everywhere. Uploads that used to fail on larger images now go through.
</Update>

<Update label="July 2026" description="Paid plans, custom questions, waitlists, and more">
  ### Starter and Pro plans
  SignUpBreeze now has paid plans for growing teams and organizations — **Starter** at \$9/mo and **Pro** at \$29/mo — each with a **30-day free trial**. See what's included and subscribe from [Settings > Billing](/account/billing).

  ### Team collaboration
  On paid plans, invite teammates into your organization and manage their roles from **Settings > Members**, so more than one person can run your events.

  ### Custom questions
  Collect exactly what you need at sign-up — t-shirt sizes, dietary needs, emergency contacts, and more. Build a [reusable question library](/organizers/custom-questions) and attach questions to any event.

  ### Waitlists
  Let volunteers [join a waitlist](/organizers/managing-waitlists) when a shift is full. When a spot opens, the next person in line is offered it — automatically or by you.

  ### Configurable reminders
  Fine-tune [when shift reminders send](/organizers/reminders) — set your own times, add or remove reminders, or turn them off — as a team-wide default or per event.

  ### Export registrations
  Download your event's registrations as a CSV, including custom-question answers, from the **Shifts & Registrations** tab.

  ### Team calendars for sports
  Coaches and team managers using **PlayMetrics** can connect their schedule so every game gets its own sign-up page for snacks and drinks — kept in sync automatically. [Reach out](mailto:hello@signupbreeze.com) to get set up.

  ### Welcome emails
  New organizers now get a welcome email, plus friendly nudges when they publish their first event and receive their first sign-up.

  ### Refreshed look
  An updated color palette across the app for a cleaner, brighter feel.
</Update>

<Update label="June 2026" description="Multiple organizations and email improvements">
  ### Multiple organizations
  Run separate workspaces from one account. Switch between your organizations from the sidebar, or create a new one in a couple of clicks.

  ### New email provider
  Transactional emails now send through Resend for improved deliverability and reliability.

  ### Refreshed Event UI for mobile
  The event management interface has been refactored for a cleaner, more responsive experience on small screens and touch devices.

  ### Earlier shift reminders
  Reminder emails now go out 48 hours before a shift, giving volunteers more lead time.
</Update>

<Update label="May 2026" description="Documentation, FAQ, and QR codes">
  ### Documentation site
  Comprehensive documentation is now live at [docs.signupbreeze.com](https://docs.signupbreeze.com). Covers getting started, organizer guides, volunteer guides, and account settings.

  ### FAQ page
  A new [FAQ page](https://signupbreeze.com/faq) answers common questions about creating events, managing signups, and how SignUpBreeze works — right on the main site.

  ### QR codes for events
  Every event now has an automatically generated QR code. Download it as a PNG for flyers, posters, and bulletins. Volunteers scan and sign up instantly.

  ### Notification settings
  New settings page to control which email notifications you receive, including a daily digest of sign-up activity.
</Update>

<Update label="April 2026" description="Dark mode and social login">
  ### Dark mode
  Full dark mode support across the entire app. Set it in Settings > Appearance, or let it follow your system preference.

  ### Social login
  Sign in with Google or Apple, alongside signing in with a one-time code sent to your email — no password required.
</Update>

<Update label="March 2026" description="Public event pages and magic links">

  ### Event management
  Create, edit, and organize events with shifts. Full dashboard for tracking volunteer signups across all your events.
