A running log of what's new in SignUpBreeze.

---

## May 2026

### QR codes for events
Every event now has an automatically generated QR code. Download it as a PNG in multiple sizes for flyers, posters, and bulletins. Volunteers scan and sign up instantly.

### Team invitations
Invite other organizers to your team via email. Invited members can accept from a link and immediately access shared events.

### Notification settings
New settings page to control which email notifications you receive.

---

## April 2026

### Dark mode
Full dark mode support across the entire app. Set it in Settings > Appearance, or let it follow your system preference.

### Two-factor authentication
Secure your organizer account with 2FA using any TOTP authenticator app. Recovery codes included.

### Social login
Sign in with Google, Apple, or Facebook in addition to email and password.

---

## March 2026

### Public event pages
Every event gets a clean, mobile-optimized public page with a shareable URL. Volunteers see shift availability and sign up without creating an account.

### Magic link registration
Volunteers sign up with just their name and email. A secure magic link is emailed for confirmation and future reference — no password needed.

### Event management
Create, edit, and organize events with shifts. Full dashboard for tracking volunteer signups across all your events.

### Organizations and teams
Workspace model for organizers. Create an organization, manage events, and prepare for team collaboration.

---

## February 2026

### SignUpBreeze launch
Initial release of SignUpBreeze — a modern, mobile-first, ad-free alternative to SignUpGenius.
